# Sahaaya – Demo Video Script (target length: about 17 minutes 30 seconds)

The challenge requires a **15–20 minute** demo video, uploaded to YouTube as **Unlisted**, with the link in the README. This script runs about 17:30 so you stay safely inside the limit even if you speak a little faster or slower.

> **Status:** the video has **not** been recorded or uploaded. After uploading, paste the real link into README section 8 (replace `PASTE_UNLISTED_YOUTUBE_LINK_HERE`).

## Before you record (checklist)

1. Start the backend: in `backend`, run `npm start`. Open `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Start LM Studio, load **Gemma 3 1B** (or Qwen), and switch on the server (port 1234). Do one test question first so the model is warm and the first answer is fast.
3. Create a **new demo account** during the recording (do not reuse a real password).
4. Open these tabs ready: the app, `docs/Sahaaya-Presentation.pdf`, `docs/Sahaaya-Architecture-Diagram.pdf`, your GitHub repository, LM Studio.
5. Close notifications and private windows. Zoom the browser to about 110%.
6. Recording tool: OBS Studio, or Windows **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar). Record screen plus microphone at 1080p if possible.
7. Say the numbers honestly: **never** call waiting time, beds or tokens "real-time" or "live". Say "simulated".

## Segment details and narration points

### 0:00–1:00 Introduction and disclaimer
- Say: project name "Sahaaya – Smart Healthcare Navigation System", tagline "Your Health. Our Support.", the challenge name, your team and college (read from README sections 2 and 3).
- Say clearly: "This is a proof-of-concept prototype. All hospital data, waiting times, beds and tokens you will see are simulated demo data. It is not connected to any live hospital system."

### 1:00–2:30 Problem and use case
- People struggle to find the right hospital or department, cannot see how busy it is, and booking, tokens and directions are in different places. Language barriers and unclear emergency steps add to this.
- Use case: patient navigation and visit planning, then target users: patients, families, caregivers.
- Mention this is a general problem framing; no survey was done.

### 2:30–4:00 Solution and architecture
- Four steps: Search, Understand (Digital Twin), Book, Go (maps and 112).
- Open the architecture diagram: browser frontend, Express backend, SQLite database, optional local LM Studio, and Google Maps links outside the app.
- Point out that LM Studio is only called by the backend on localhost and is not exposed publicly, and that no API keys are used.

### 4:00–5:30 Home and search
- Show the Home screen and the amber demo-data note.
- Search `cardiology`, then type the sentence `Which hospital has cardiology?`, then `blood test`. Explain that the backend search understands department and service names.
- Open Hospitals List, use the Government and Private filters, and clear the search.
- Show the Nearby Hospitals "Est. wait ... (simulated)" line.

### 5:30–7:30 Hospital Details and Digital Twin
- Open Sri Sai Hospital: departments, doctors, services, sample timings.
- Scroll to the Digital Twin card titled "Digital Twin — Prototype Simulation". Explain each tile: people waiting (est.), estimated wait, beds free (simulated), doctors on duty (simulated), crowding level, 2-hour trend.
- Explain what is simulated (walk-in queue, beds, doctors from a daily demand curve with peaks near 11 AM and 6 PM) and what is real (tokens booked through Sahaaya are added to the queue).
- Briefly open `backend/twin.js` and show the `simulate()` function. Say that a real deployment would replace this one function with verified hospital feeds.
- Say that no machine-learning model is used for the twin.

### 7:30–9:00 Explain with AI
- Click **Explain with AI**. Wait for the answer (first call can take longer while the model loads).
- Point to the label "AI-generated by local model (name). AI can make mistakes".
- Switch to LM Studio: show the server running, the loaded model and the log of the request.
- Explain: no training or fine-tuning, prompt design only, hospital facts and twin numbers are added to the prompt, models tried in order Gemma, Qwen, Llama, small models make mistakes.

### 9:00–11:30 Accounts, booking and tokens
- Click Book Appointment while logged out: show that it asks you to log in and keeps your choices.
- Sign up with a demo account. Say that passwords are stored as scrypt hashes, never in plain text, and that this is a prototype so no real password should be used.
- Book: hospital, department, doctor, date, time slot. Show the confirmation and token (for example G10).
- Open Appointments & Tracking: token status, "ahead of you in the Sahaaya prototype queue", View details, then Cancel.
- Open Token Allocation: choose Today, a hospital and department, click Check Availability. Explain demo capacity of 40 per department per day minus tokens booked through Sahaaya, and that tokens are not registered with any hospital.
- Optional: stop and restart the backend and reload to show the data is still there.

### 11:30–13:00 Assistant and emergency
- On Voice & Language, use the example questions in the Sahaaya Assistant.
- Type `I have chest pain`. Show the red box and **Call 112** button. Explain that emergency wording is handled by a fixed rule and the AI model is never asked, and that the app does not diagnose.
- Mention that emergency phrases are covered in five languages.

### 13:00–14:30 Languages, voice, maps, pharmacy and labs
- Open Multi-Language and switch to Telugu, then Hindi, then back to English. Say that translations cover main labels only and need native-speaker review.
- Voice search: click the microphone (Chrome or Edge), say "cardiology". Mention it uses the browser's speech recognition.
- Open Map View: choose a hospital and Car, Bike or Walk, click **Navigate in Google Maps**. Say that the map picture is a labelled demo with no live GPS or traffic, and that the buttons open real Google Maps directions without any API key.
- Open Pharmacy & Labs and say the information is sample data and ordering is not connected.

### 14:30–15:30 AI-off fallback
- In LM Studio, stop the server.
- Ask the Assistant "Which hospital has cardiology?". Show the keyword answer and the label "local AI is unavailable in this environment".
- Click **Explain with AI**: show the automatic summary.
- Type `I have chest pain` again: the emergency warning still works. The app does not crash.

### 15:30–16:30 Code and repository tour
- Show the public GitHub repository: `frontend`, `backend`, `docs`, README, LICENSE.
- Scroll through the README sections: mandatory items index, team, college, AI details, demo-data table.
- Show the `.gitignore` and mention that `node_modules` and the database file are not in the repository.
- Mention the MIT license.

### 16:30–17:30 Outcomes, limitations, future scope and close
- Observed: all 11 screens work; booking, token and cancel work and survive a restart; the local AI answered in about 5 seconds on the development laptop (informal); emergency and fallback behaviour work. There was no real-world pilot or user study.
- Expected (not measured): faster discovery, fewer wasted trips, clearer emergency path, better local-language access.
- Limitations: simulated data, not connected to live hospital systems, small models make mistakes.
- Future scope (not built): verified real hospital data, OpenStreetMap-based location, real hospital APIs and real-time availability where officially available, better multilingual voice, production security, cloud deployment.
- Close with the disclaimer again, the tagline, and thank the viewers.

## If the video is too short or too long

- **Under 15 minutes:** spend longer on the code tour (show `twin.js`, `ai.js`, `routes/ai.js`), on the presentation slides, and on the AI-off demo.
- **Over 20 minutes:** shorten the introduction and the problem slides, and skip the optional restart demonstration.

## Upload to YouTube as Unlisted (exact steps)

1. Sign in at youtube.com, click **Create**, then **Upload video**.
2. Select the video file. Enter the title "Sahaaya – Smart Healthcare Navigation System – Demo".
3. Answer the audience question ("Made for kids": No).
4. On the **Visibility** step choose **Unlisted** (not Private, not Public). Click **Save**.
5. Copy the video link (it looks like `https://youtu.be/...`).
6. Open the link in a **private/incognito window** where you are not signed in. It must play. If it asks you to sign in, the visibility is wrong.
7. Check the video length shows between 15:00 and 20:00.
8. Paste the link into README section 8, replacing `PASTE_UNLISTED_YOUTUBE_LINK_HERE`, then commit the change on GitHub.
